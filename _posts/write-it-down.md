---
title: "Write it down"
date: "2026-08-30"
excerpt: "LLMs love text. So write."
---

A pen and paper or a doc. That’s it. Building a presentation? Write it down first. In most cases, skip the presentation all together. The gradual buildup of complexity in organizations unnecessarily makes things harder. Countless hours spent on building presentations that no one will read.

Writing has a deeper connection to how we understand information. It feels natural. Powerpoint feels off. It misses the full context.

As AI becomes more mainstream, the need to write is even more important. LLMs understand text. So write in a doc or markdown.

Don’t let AI do all the writing. Writing is thinking.

An entire company corpus should be in text. That’s where AI can dig in. Summarize documents for colleagues. Build new material from text.

Text is the foundation. Write.
